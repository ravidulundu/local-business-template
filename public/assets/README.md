# Ventos & Acos Visual Assets

All website imagery is local to this directory. Do not replace these files with remote image URLs.

Current generated assets used by the landing page:

- `hero-atelier.webp` - wide cinematic hero image for a premium private atelier in Salvador, with white Candomble ceremonial clothing, white torco, gold accessories, fabrics and warm dark atelier lighting.
- `roupa-santo-premium.webp` - finished premium roupa de santo with white lace, layered skirt, ivory fabric and handmade finishing.
- `conjunto-branco-dourado.webp` - white and gold ceremonial garment with ivory fabric and refined gold embroidery.
- `conjunto-vermelho-branco.webp` - deep red and white ceremonial garment, grounded and respectful.
- `detalhe-renda.webp` - macro close-up of white lace fabric and handmade textile detail.
- `detalhe-bordado-ouro.webp` - macro close-up of gold embroidery on ivory fabric.
- `costureira-trabalhando.webp` - hands of a seamstress working on white lace ceremonial garment with tools and measuring tape.
- `tecidos-premium.webp` - ivory, white, gold, bronze and deep red fabrics arranged in a private atelier.
- `fios-contas-guias.webp` - close-up of traditional bead strands and accessories in white, gold, red and bronze tones.
- `simbolo-ventos-acos.webp` - abstract symbolic image with red fabric movement and subtle iron or steel craft element, without deity depiction.

Legacy generated files from earlier drafts may remain in this folder but are not referenced by the current page.
